Flashcards

Two options, one tap

Pick one of two and find out immediately. Many cards are a flowchart, a comparison table or a diagram to read rather than a paragraph, and Learn more opens the explanation without slowing the run down.

Spaced repetition

SM-2 scheduling

Cards you miss come back the same session and again tomorrow. Cards you know stretch out to weeks, so revision time goes where it is actually needed.

Scored quiz

Drill with a score

Multiple choice with per-topic breakdown, a review of everything you missed, and answers that still feed the review schedule.

Mock interview

Say it out loud

Open-ended questions with no options to pick from. Answer aloud, then compare against a strong spoken answer and grade yourself honestly.

Lessons

Learn the topic first

Around 12,000 words of deep-dive explainers written the way you would explain the concept in an interview, not the way a textbook would.

Focus

Topic and difficulty filters

Select the topics you want to become expert at and the difficulty band you want to work in. Every mode draws from that selection.

Offline

Works on a plane

The whole question bank and every lesson ship inside the APK. Only the news feed needs a connection, and it caches the last fetch.

What is covered

  • ML Fundamentals — bias-variance, leakage, calibration, drift
  • Deep Learning — backprop, normalization, optimizers, parallelism
  • Transformers & LLMs — attention, KV cache, MoE, quantization
  • RAG & Vector Search — chunking, hybrid search, HNSW, reranking
  • Fine-Tuning & Alignment — LoRA, RLHF, DPO, RLVR
  • Evaluation & Metrics — LLM-as-judge, A/B tests, Goodhart
  • MLOps & Serving — skew, continuous batching, cost levers
  • AI System Design — retrieve-then-rank, agents, multi-tenancy
